# Jukebox Voice Command Cheat Sheet

**How to Use the Microphone**
- **Push-to-Talk:** Press and hold the button on your center console, speak your command clearly, and then release the button.
- Wait a second or two for the system to process your request and start playing.

---

## 🎵 Basic Playback
*To play music, just ask for the artist, album, genre, or track. By default, music plays in your folder number order.*

- **"Play classical"** *(Plays all classical albums in folder order)*
- **"Play Bruce Springsteen"**
- **"Play The Rising"**
- **"Play some jazz"** *(The word "some" automatically shuffles the music)*

## 🔀 Shuffling Music
*If you don't want to listen in folder order, tell the Jukebox to shuffle.*

- **"Shuffle all rock music"**
- **"Shuffle Michael Bolton"**
- **"Shuffle my music"** *(Shuffles everything in the catalog)*

## ⏯️ Pausing and Stopping
*Control the current playback.*

- **"Pause"** *(Pauses the current song)*
- **"Resume"** *(Continues playing where you left off)*
- **"Stop"** *(Stops playback completely and clears the queue)*

## ⏭️ Skipping Tracks and Albums
*If you don't like what's currently playing, you can skip ahead. By default, saying "skip" will skip to the next **album**.*

- **"Skip"** or **"Next"** *(Skips the rest of the current album and starts the next one)*
- **"Skip this track"** *(Skips only the current song and plays the next song on the album)*
- **"Skip this artist"** *(Skips the rest of the current artist's albums and moves to the next artist in the queue)*

---

## 💡 Pro Tips
- **Keep it simple:** The AI is smart, but short and clear commands work best (e.g., "Play the Eagles" instead of "I would like to listen to the Eagles right now").
- **Folder Sorting:** Your music is sorted by the physical numbers you put at the start of your folder names (e.g. `1 Bach`, `2 Beethoven`). When you ask for a genre, it will play through those albums in numerical order!
